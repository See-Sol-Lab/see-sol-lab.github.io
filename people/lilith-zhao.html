Biography

Lilith Zhao has an academic background in communication at Tsinghua University and sociology at the University of Edinburgh. She previously worked at CCID and the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology (CAICT), with a focus on artificial intelligence policy research.

Her current work examines persistent AI agents, memory architectures, state continuity across context boundaries, and long-term human–AI collaboration. At See Sol Lab, she leads research framing, evidence review, public-accountability decisions, and human-side authorship and editorial responsibility.
